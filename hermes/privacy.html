Privacy policy

Hermes personal dashboard · Last updated November 4, 2026

This policy describes the site owner's private assistant and dashboard, not EdgeRent's rental services. The Google integration is intended for the owner's own account, not for other users.

Data used and purpose

With the owner's Google authorization, the assistant accesses Gmail messages and metadata to search, read, draft, send, and organize email on request. It accesses Google Calendar events to show the owner's schedule on a home-network dashboard and to manage events when requested. It does not access Google data for advertising or sell it.

Storage and processing

Google OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's Windows computer, not in this public website. The local dashboard may temporarily cache calendar event details and display them to devices on the owner's home network. When the owner asks the AI assistant to work with email or calendar content, relevant data may be sent to the AI provider configured for the assistant to process that request. This public information page does not itself request Google account access.

Control and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's Google access in Google Account security settings. Local credentials and locally stored dashboard data can be deleted from the owner's computer. For questions or deletion assistance, contact the site owner directly.
